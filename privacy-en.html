This policy explains how information is handled in StoryPiece.

This policy applies to StoryPiece, including pre-release test versions. Age settings and ad consent features are available after updating to a version that supports them.

1. Photos, text, and storage on your device

The app stores the photos you select, titles, text, dates, tags, albums, and settings on your device to create, organize, display, and export your memories. It saves copies of photos for use in the app. The app's implementation does not send these photos or text to the provider's servers or to the advertising SDK. The app does not have its own account registration, cloud synchronization, or public posting features.

Backup and synchronization features provided by your device, operating system, or chosen file destination follow their respective settings and service policies. Storage on your device does not guarantee that the operating system or other services will never back up the data.

2. Backups and exports

You can export backups containing photos and text, card images, and album HTML files. You choose where to save files or which other apps to send them to. If you share files with others, the recipients may be able to view their contents. The app does not provide encryption or password protection for backup files. Choose storage locations and sharing permissions carefully.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). For ad delivery, ad measurement, service improvement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, Google may collect and share IP addresses, device and account identifiers, information about interactions with ads and other content, and diagnostic information. An approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. These SDK communications are protected by TLS. Photos and memory text are not attached to ad requests.

Current ad requests are marked as non-personalized. This does not mean that device information and other data are no longer collected. For information about Google's handling of data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
  • Google's advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads?hl=en

You can manage your device's advertising ID settings in Android settings.

To handle ads according to age, the app asks for an age group: under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to answer. No date of birth is collected. The choice is stored in device settings and is not included in memory backup ZIPs. For users aged 13 or older, ad requests depend on age-appropriate restrictions and consent status. No ads are requested for users under 13 or those who do not answer. You can change the age group in Settings. Google consent information is updated at app launch, and a consent form is shown when required. If Google requires a way to change consent choices, Ad privacy choices appears in Settings. Choosing an age group does not mean consenting to ads.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete memories in the app. However, removing a memory from the list does not guarantee immediate or complete erasure of related files on the device. To erase the stored information managed by the app together, you can clear this app's storage or data in Android settings. Back up any records you need before doing so.

Manage and delete exported files, copies held by other apps or the operating system, and the original photos separately. Retention and deletion of advertising-related information handled by Google follow Google's policies.

5. Contact

Contact email: storypiece.support@gmail.com

When contacting us, provide only the information needed to answer your inquiry. The provider uses that information to respond to your inquiry. You do not need to send passwords, verification codes, identity documents, or photos or text from your personal memories.

6. Changes

This policy will be updated as features and services change. Important changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
